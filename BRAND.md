# Cap7CE Brand and Naming Policy

Cap7CE is a source-available project maintained by 7C93F3-L. This policy distinguishes software-license rights from project identity and does not expand the rights granted by PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.

## Covered project identity

The following identify the official Cap7CE project:

- The name `Cap7CE`.
- The Cap7CE logo and application icon.
- Signature graphics and official visual identity assets.
- Names or graphics that are confusingly similar and imply an official release.

No claim is made here that any mark is formally registered. This policy reserves the project's branding and prevents modified builds from being presented as official Cap7CE releases.

## Allowed uses

You may:

- Refer to Cap7CE accurately in articles, reviews, comparisons, package descriptions, and compatibility statements.
- State that a project is based on, derived from, or compatible with Cap7CE.
- Redistribute an unmodified official build while preserving its version, license, attribution, and origin.
- Link to the official repository and use the name in ordinary factual discussion.

## Modified versions and forks

If you distribute a modified build or fork:

- Use a distinct product name and icon.
- Clearly state that the project is unofficial and derived from Cap7CE.
- Do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, maintenance, or release by the official Cap7CE project.
- Do not use the official logo or signature as the primary identity of the modified product.

These naming rules do not prevent you from describing compatibility or origin, and they do not change your rights under the software license.
